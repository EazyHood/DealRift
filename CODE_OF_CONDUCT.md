# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DealRift contributors are expected to communicate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation, and publication of another person's private information are not.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports should be made privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile when possible. Do not disclose sensitive reports in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected participants.
