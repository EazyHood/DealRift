# DealRift brand

Source: the existing application, preserved for the personal assistant update.

- Dark navy surfaces and cool, readable text.
- Mint/cyan is the primary action and focus accent. Amber indicates upcoming or limited data; red is reserved for errors and destructive actions.
- Keep the existing system sans-serif typography, compact rows and game imagery.
- Use clear Spanish and English copy, with evidence and dates beside recommendations.
- Prefer restrained transitions, respect reduced motion, and keep the animated radar optional.
- Prioritize the game list and personal library over decorative metrics.
